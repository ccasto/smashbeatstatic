Smash Beat — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Smash Beat ("the app") is a block-puzzle game for Android published by Corrado Casto ("we"), an independent developer based in Italy. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and what your choices are. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own: it never asks for your name, e-mail address or any other personal details.

1. Data stored on your device

Your best score, your settings (sound, vibration, effects intensity, gem symbols) and your tutorial progress are saved locally on your device. They never leave it and we have no access to them. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from Android settings, deletes them.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and funded by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC and, for users in the European Economic Area, Google Ireland Limited. Three ad formats are used: a banner during play, a full-screen ad between runs, and an optional rewarded video that you choose to watch to continue a run ("Revive").

To select, deliver and measure ads, and to detect fraud, Google receives and processes:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID of your device (resettable) and, where available, Android Privacy Sandbox signals such as TopicsAd selection, frequency capping, measurement
Approximate location derived from your IP address (country / region)Serving ads relevant to your region and complying with local law
Device and app information: model, OS version, language, screen size, app version, ad interaction eventsAd delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and diagnostics

Google acts as an independent controller for this data. How it is used is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the AdMob data disclosure.

Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before any ad is requested the app shows Google's consent form. You can decline personalised advertising; ads are then non-personalised or, if you decline everything, not shown at all. You can review or change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy & ads consent inside the app.
  • Everywhere: Android lets you reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ads personalisation under Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).

Legal basis (GDPR): your consent for personalised ads and for storing identifiers on your device; our legitimate interest in funding a free app through non-personalised ads and in preventing ad fraud.

3. Gameplay data (optional)

The app has a Settings → Share gameplay data switch, off by default. When it is on, the app may record anonymous gameplay events (scores, levels reached, lines cleared, tutorial steps completed) to help us balance the game's difficulty. These events contain no personal information and no identifiers. In the current version of the app they are not transmitted anywhere; if a future version starts sending them to an analytics service, this policy will be updated first and the switch will remain off by default.

4. Sharing your score

The "Share score" button hands a short text containing your score to the Android share sheet. Nothing is sent by the app itself: the text goes only to the app you choose (messaging, social network, etc.), which then handles it under its own privacy policy.

5. Permissions

The app requests network access (to load ads), the Android advertising-ID permission (used by AdMob as described above) and no other sensitive permission: no camera, microphone, contacts, precise location or storage access.

6. International transfers

Google may process advertising data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Google relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses for such transfers; see Google's business data responsibility page.

7. Data retention

We keep no data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain. Data processed by Google is retained according to Google's retention policy.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in some EU countries) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The app is rated for a general audience and is published on Google Play for users aged 13 and over.

9. Your rights

If you are in the EEA or UK you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali). Because the app itself holds no personal data about you, requests concerning advertising data should be addressed to Google through My Ad Center or the Google Privacy Policy. You can still write to us at the address below and we will help you route the request.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with an updated date. Material changes are also announced in the app's release notes.
